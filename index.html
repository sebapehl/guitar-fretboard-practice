Fretboard Hacks

🐙 The Octopus (Octaves)

Find one note, find them all. Every note has "twins" (octaves) in predictable spots. Tap a fret to see the patterns:

The Standard (2-2 Rule)
On E and A strings: Move 2 strings down and 2 frets up.
The B-String Shift (+1)
If your jump lands on the B or high E: Move 3 frets up instead of 2.

🎯 The Anchor Strings (E & A)

Don't memorize 132 notes. Just learn the Low E and A strings. These are your "GPS" anchors. Tap a note on the bottom two strings to see how they unlock the rest of the neck.

Pro Tip: 100% of standard Power Chords use these two strings as their root!

🪜 The Major Scale Ladder

Every major scale follows a simple math formula: Whole-Whole-Half-Whole-Whole-Whole-Half. Tap any fret to build a scale "ladder" along that single string.
