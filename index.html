THE WARRIOR'S CODE

No shortcuts. No excuses. Just you, the wood, and the clock. FretboardBattle is a competitive training arena designed for guitarists who want to stop guessing and start knowing the neck.

Mastery Elements

  • ⚔️ Speed Drills: Identify notes from random fret positions under pressure.
  • 🎯 Target Search: Locate given notes across specific strings and neighborhoods.
  • 🐙 Theory Hacks: Interactive visual landcards like "The Octopus" to unlock octave and scale patterns.
  • 🏆 Hall of Fame: Compete for the top Mastery Score on global and daily leaderboards.

Progress vs. Competition

FretboardBattle tracks your growth through two distinct systems. Understanding the difference is key to your training:

📈 Levels (Self-Improvement) This is your Warrior's Journey. Every single correct note adds to your XP. Levels represent your total dedication and "time on the wood." You never lose XP; you only grow stronger.
⚔️ Leaderboards (Community Arena) This is the Competitive Arena. It ranks your absolute best "Perfect Rounds" against other players. It's about peak performance, speed, and precision in a single moment.

The XP & Leveling System

Every session earns you XP, propelling you through the ranks of mastery. Here is how your progress is calculated:

  • Base Hit: +10 XP for every correct note.
  • Speed Bonus: Up to +15 XP for fast identifies (sub-3 second reactions).
  • Complexity Bonus: Practice wider fret ranges to multiply your gains.
  • Perfect Round: A massive +50 XP bonus for a flawless 10/10 performance.

Fretboard Theory

🐙 The Octopus (Octaves)

Find one note, find them all. Every note has "twins" (octaves) in predictable spots. Tap a fret to see the patterns:

The Standard (2-2 Rule)
On E and A strings: Move 2 strings down and 2 frets up.
The B-String Shift (+1)
If your jump lands on the B or high E: Move 3 frets up instead of 2.

🎯 The Anchor Strings (E & A)

Don't memorize 132 notes. Just learn the Low E and A strings. These are your "GPS" anchors. Tap a note on the bottom two strings to see how they unlock the rest of the neck.

Pro Tip: 100% of standard Power Chords use these two strings as their root!

🪜 The Major Scale Map

Every major scale follows a simple math formula: Whole-Whole-Half-Whole-Whole-Whole-Half. Tap any fret to see how that scale maps across the entire fretboard.

Tip: The scale pattern repeats every 12 frets and forms the basis for all chords and solos.

How rankings work

Your rank is calculated using your Mastery Score, which factors in both your speed and the difficulty of your chosen fret range. Because searching a larger part of the neck is exponentially harder, your fret range acts as a significant multiplier. Your rank title (e.g., Grandmaster, Sensei) is then determined by how your score compares to the entire FretboardBattle community.
